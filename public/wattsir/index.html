Wattsir

Smarter Energy. A Greener Tomorrow.  |  Daha Akıllı Enerji. Daha Yeşil Bir Yarın.

Wattsir is a 100% offline-first electricity consumption and cost calculator supporting 13 languages and 25 currencies. Estimate kWh usage, compare appliances side by side, manage household devices, and export portable JSON backups — with no account required.
