Privacy Policy – YASSELIM

Effective Date: November 22, 2026  |  Package ID: com.yasselim.yasselim  |  Contact: mustafasirin42@gmail.com

1. Overview & Offline-First Philosophy

YASSELIM ("the App") is an offline-first, private personal journaling and reflective writing application. Your private thoughts, moods, and unsent letters belong exclusively to you.

  • No User Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email address to use YASSELIM.
  • No Cloud Synchronization: Your journal entries, mood logs, tags, and unsent letters are never uploaded to external servers or cloud databases.
  • No AI Training: Your private writings are never analyzed, scanned, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

2. Local On-Device Storage & Encryption at Rest

All personal writings created in YASSELIM are stored exclusively on your local device and encrypted at rest using SHA-256 key derivation, stream encryption, and HMAC-SHA256 authentication. Optional PIN lock protection secures local access on your device.

3. Data Collection & Sharing (Google Play Data Safety)

  • Personal Data Collected: None. We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or personal writings.
  • Third-Party Data Sharing: None. Your personal entries are never transmitted, shared, or sold to third parties.
  • Writing Editor Immunity: No advertisements are ever displayed inside the Writing Editor screen.

4. User Control & Data Deletion (GDPR & KVKK)

  • Export & Backup: You can export your entries as encrypted backup files or PDF documents at any time into a folder of your choice.
  • Instant Deletion: You can permanently delete all local entries, letters, and preferences at any time from Settings → Delete All Local Data, or by uninstalling the App.

5. Medical & Psychological Disclaimer

YASSELIM is strictly a personal writing and journaling application. It does not provide medical advice, psychological therapy, clinical diagnosis, or crisis intervention.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
