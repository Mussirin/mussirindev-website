Privacy Policy – Wattsir

Effective Date: November 28, 2026  |  Package ID: com.wattsir.app  |  Developer: Mustafa Şirin (mussirindev)  |  Contact: mustafasirin42@gmail.com

1. Overview & 100% Offline-First Design

Wattsir (Smarter Energy. A Greener Tomorrow.) is an offline-first household electricity consumption and cost calculator supporting 13 languages and 25 global currencies.

  • No User Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or submit an email address or password to use Wattsir.
  • No Developer Cloud Backend: Wattsir operates without external developer servers. Your appliances, tariff rates, and calculation records are never uploaded to developer cloud databases.

2. Local On-Device SQLite Storage

All user-entered energy records remain exclusively on your device inside a local SQLite database managed via Drift:

  • Application Preferences (AppSettingsTable): Electricity price per kWh, selected currency, decimal precision, theme mode, language choice, and onboarding status.
  • Saved Appliances (SavedDevicesTable): Appliance names, categories, calculation modes (Continuous, Per Cycle, Flexible), wattage ratings, durations, cycles, custom notes, and timestamps.
  • Calculation History Snapshots (CalculationHistoryTable): Immutable energy (kWh) and cost results saved by you alongside the active tariff rate at the time of calculation.
  • Local Pro Entitlement Cache (EntitlementCacheTable): Locally cached product identifier, verification source, purchase timestamp, and expiration date so Wattsir Pro continues working offline.

Android Device Backup Exclusion: Android manifest rules (android:allowBackup="false" and android:fullBackupContent="false") prevent automatic OS cloud backups of Wattsir's local SQLite database without your explicit action.

3. Manual User-Initiated JSON Backup & Restore

Wattsir provides a strictly manual, user-triggered JSON backup tool (wattsir_backup format):

  • A backup file is generated only when you explicitly tap Export or Share Backup in Settings.
  • Exported files contain your local settings, saved appliances, and calculation history — never payment credentials or Pro entitlement tokens.
  • You maintain full control over where exported files are saved or shared via your operating system's native file picker or share sheet.

4. Advertising & Consent – Free Version Only

The Free version of Wattsir integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK (including Android com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission) to display non-intrusive ads when online:

  • Ad-Free Calculators & Offline Support: Ads never appear on the active Calculator or Calculation Result screens, and offline features never block waiting for ad networks.
  • Wattsir Pro (Ad-Free): Upgrading to Wattsir Pro disables all ad requests and removes all ads from the application.
  • Data Processed by Google AdMob: While online on the Free tier, Google AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse IP-based location, ad impression and interaction events, and performance/crash diagnostics.
  • User Consent: Where required by regional regulations (GDPR in the EEA/UK and US state privacy frameworks), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent choices are respected. Review Google's privacy disclosures at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Wattsir offers optional paid upgrades (**Wattsir Pro** via wattsir_pro_monthly, wattsir_pro_annual, and one-time wattsir_pro_lifetime) processed officially through Google Play Billing and Apple StoreKit.

  • Wattsir never collects, views, or stores your credit card numbers, billing address, or store passwords.
  • Purchase tokens are used strictly to verify and restore Pro entitlements with Google Play or Apple App Store.

6. Data Retention, Deletion & User Control

Because Wattsir stores no personal account data on developer servers, you have instant control over all data on your device:

  • Delete individual saved appliances or calculation history entries directly inside the app.
  • Clear all calculation history in one step or overwrite data with a verified JSON backup.
  • Clear app storage in your OS settings or uninstall Wattsir to permanently delete the local SQLite database and all stored preferences.

7. Utility Estimation Disclaimer & Children's Privacy

Wattsir is an informational household utility estimation calculator designed to help users model appliance energy costs using typical ratings and user-entered tariffs. It is not an official utility billing meter and provides no banking or financial services.

Wattsir is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

2. Cihaz İçi Yerel SQLite Depolama

Girdiğiniz tüm veriler yalnızca kendi cihazınızdaki yerel SQLite veritabanında (Drift) tutulur:

  • Uygulama Ayarları (AppSettingsTable): kWh birim fiyatınız, para birimi seçiminiz, ondalık hassasiyetiniz, tema ve dil tercihleriniz.
  • Kayıtlı Cihazlar (SavedDevicesTable): Eklediğiniz cihaz adları, kategoriler, hesaplama modları, watt/çevrim değerleri ve notlarınız.
  • Hesaplama Geçmişi (CalculationHistoryTable): Kaydettiğiniz sabit kWh ve maliyet sonuçları ile hesaplama anındaki tarife bilgileri.
  • Pro Yetki Önbelleği (EntitlementCacheTable): Çevrimdışı Pro kullanımının sürmesi için yalnızca cihazınızda tutulan ürün kimliği ve doğrulama özeti.

Android tarafında android:allowBackup="false" ve android:fullBackupContent="false" tanımları sayesinde yerel veritabanınız izniniz olmadan otomatik bulut yedeklemesine dahil edilmez.

3. Manuel Kullanıcı Yedeklemesi ve Geri Yükleme

Yalnızca Ayarlar menüsünden kendiniz başlattığınızda cihaz ayarlarınızı, cihazlarınızı ve hesaplama geçmişinizi içeren bir wattsir_backup JSON dosyası oluşturulur. Bu dosya ödeme veya kart bilgisi içermez; dosyanın nereye kaydedileceği tamamen cihazınızın dosya/paylaşım ekranından sizin tarafınızdan belirlenir.
