Privacy Policy – Freshso

Effective Date: November 25, 2026  |  Package ID: com.freshso.app  |  Contact: mustafasirin42@gmail.com

1. Overview & Offline-First Design

Freshso (Breathe. Focus. Reset.) is a minimalist offline wellness and productivity application providing guided breathing exercises and a distraction-free focus timer.

  • No User Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or share your name, email address, or personal profile to use Freshso.
  • No Cloud Backend: Freshso operates without external developer servers. Your breathing sessions, focus timer logs, and statistics are never uploaded to developer cloud databases.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All wellness and productivity records remain locally on your device using on-device storage:

  • Completed breathing and focus session records (exercise type, duration, timestamp)
  • Local daily streak and summary statistics
  • App preferences (language, sound, haptic feedback, daily goal, reminder time, theme preference, and local Pro unlock state)

Because data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling Freshso permanently removes your local history unless backed up automatically by your operating system (such as Android Auto Backup or iCloud Backup).

3. Advertising & User Consent (Free Version)

The free version of Freshso may display non-intrusive banner advertisements on select screens via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) (never during active breathing or focus sessions).

  • Where required by regional privacy laws (such as GDPR in the EEA/UK), Freshso uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and respect your consent choices.
  • Google AdMob may process device or advertising identifiers and basic ad diagnostics strictly in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and your consent preferences.
  • You can review or update your ad privacy choices anytime from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. Upgrading to Freshso Pro disables advertisements completely.

4. In-App Purchases

One-time Pro upgrades (freshso_pro_lifetime) and purchase restorations are processed securely by Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple StoreKit (iOS). Freshso never collects or stores payment card details or billing credentials.

5. Notifications & Permissions

If you opt in to Daily Reminders or Focus completion alerts, Freshso schedules local system notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) directly on your device. No remote push-notification servers track your activity.

6. General Wellness Disclaimer & Children's Privacy

Freshso provides general breathing and focus tools for everyday relaxation and productivity. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Stop any breathing exercise immediately if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.

Freshso is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
